# Authored AML labeling protocol v1

The target is the presence of at least one authored episode moving or concealing criminal proceeds. It is not suspicion, fraud, high turnover, rule violation, or a risk-score threshold. Confirmed lawful scenarios have integer `aml_label=0`; confirmed scenarios containing such an episode have integer `aml_label=1`. Unresolved scenarios have `aml_label=null` and never enter supervised fitting.

The machine-readable protocol at `config/ml/aml-classifier-v1-protocol.json` preserves the research P01?P10 families, F01?F25 observable/context factors, and F26?F32 unsupported constraints. These factors are hypotheses and limitations, not a count-based decision rule. Salary, spending, and pauses cannot determine or discount the target. The artificial confirmed population is balanced at 50%; it makes no claim about bank prevalence.

Each JSONL record separates `public_snapshot` (financial configuration, v10 context dictionary, and complete steps) from `author_truth`, label, rationale, hypothesis source, alternative explanation, required facts, and forbidden information. Extractors must receive only the public snapshot; identifiers, party names, investigation text, review, split, labels, and old risk are excluded from model inputs. Scoped records support an obligation only for its participants, operations, amount, time, and purpose. A documented opening balance is present in both classes and does not establish the source of later incoming funds.

The pilot contains four lawful, four AML, and four unresolved cases per family (120 total). Four substantive obligation variants share a provenance group across alternative outcomes. The fourth confirmed contrast in each family has identical public observations under opposite hidden outcomes: retain this collision, report its ambiguity, and keep the group together in any future split. Unresolved cases explicitly preserve missing origin/obligation information. Family descriptions include refunds with an original advance, owned asset sales, cash needs, recurring obligations, cross-border support, owned crypto realization, reactivated accounts, and independently assessed salary/background flows. They do not claim a multi-account graph, ownership network, trade documents, blockchain trace, device telemetry, or control-evasion intent.

All cases remain `authored_unreviewed`. An independent domain reviewer must record reviewer, method, ISO calendar review date, and rationale; automated structural checks are not human expertise. Independent review must assess actual source, obligations, contrary explanation, scope, and observability. Do not convert ambiguous confirmed outcomes into confidently observable labels. Rejected cases stay outside supervised fitting. No release or bulk training is authorized until independent domain review and final v10 engine/context verification are complete.

Reproduce with `python -m scripts.aml_dataset.aml_casebook`; audit with `python -m scripts.check_aml_casebook`; test with `python -m pytest -q tests/unit/test_aml_label_contract.py`. The financial audit calls the same v10 evaluator as the game (`services.aml_context.evaluate`), preserving context and purpose selections during canonical validation and enforcing `submit_blockers`, target 400000 and max 14 actions. Unknown evidence parties and reversed validity scopes fail the audit. No score or rubric is evaluated. The audit reports `financial_contract=v10` and `v10_verification=passed` only when no structural/resource errors exist. Financial feasibility precedes UI work; a failed legitimate plot must be reported, not repaired through permissive validation or relabeling. Independent domain review remains a closed release gate.

Each material payment now has a directional `money-N` claim, separate scoped fact and original event record naming amount, participants and prior event. Asset/crypto source claims cover credits only; separate personal obligations explain debits. Cash claims have no invented counterparty. P10 variants use their actual shared-expense/refund/loan/personal-spending purposes. P09 contains a complete empty 30-day history. `economic_records` and `expected_evidence` remain outside the public snapshot: they are author/reviewer material, not model evidence. Resolver tests independently require 240000 RUB credit and 400000 RUB debit coverage for known lawful routes and check exact contradicted-step application in observed AML contrasts.

## Зафиксированный независимый review — 17 сентября 2026

Все120 пилотных кейсов приняты после трёх проходов отдельного агента. Это предметная проверка ИИ, не заключение человека-эксперта. Исходный `pilot/casebook.jsonl` остаётся authored_unreviewed для воспроизведения генератора; `pilot/reviewed-casebook.jsonl` содержит принятые записи и reviewer/method/date/rationale. `pilot/domain-review.json` связывает решения с SHA-256 исходного файла и каждого объекта. Импорт через `scripts.aml_dataset.aml_review.apply_review` отклоняет изменённые хеши, неполное покрытие, отклонённые кейсы и самопроверку.
